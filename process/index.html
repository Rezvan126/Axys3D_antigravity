Process / Delivery controls

A production workflow with visible review gates.

The process separates production activity from design authority and keeps the source revision, approver, issue status and handoff record visible at every stage.

01

Qualification and package definition

Confirm intended use, source coverage, programme, commercial assumptions and whether the requested work fits the production-support boundary.

02

Information and responsibility audit

Review the BEP or equivalent protocol, model ownership, LOD/LOI, coordinates, formats, approvals, NDA and rights status.

03

Production plan and representative sample

Break scope into controlled tasks, name checkers and approvers, and validate a representative output before scaling production.

04

Production and technical QA

Author, coordinate or visualize against the approved source revision with task-level checks and recorded assumptions.

05

Client-led review and change control

Route design and discipline decisions to authorized leads; update only from approved instructions and retain revision traceability.

06

Delivery and structured handoff

Issue agreed formats with status, open items, limitations, rights/credits and the receiving team's validation record.

Control points

Nothing moves downstream on an implied approval.

  • Authoritative source revision named
  • Responsible reviewer identified
  • Decision and assumptions recorded
  • Rights and publication status checked
  • Open items retained at handoff
  • Professional authority remains client-led
