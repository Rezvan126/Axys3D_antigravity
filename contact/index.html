Scoping checklist

Information needed before capacity is proposed.

  • Project type, stage and disciplines
  • Available Revit/BIM/CAD/model inputs
  • BEP, naming, classification and model standards
  • LOD/LOI or information-delivery requirements
  • Federation, clash-review and issue-management process
  • Visualization, animation or real-time use case
  • Review milestones, responsible approvers and target dates
  • Required native files, exports, registers and handoff package
